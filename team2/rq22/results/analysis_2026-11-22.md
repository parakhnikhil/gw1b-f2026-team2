## Provisional bootstrap at 1.15B parameters and 23B tokens

Reproduce from the repository root with `python3 -u team2/rq22_bootstrap.py`.

| Fit | Point estimate | Bootstrap median | 90% bootstrap range | Fits at a parameter bound |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original 50M | 2.3414 | 2.3437 | 2.2895–2.4267 | 43/300 |
| Alternate 576×9 | 2.3713 | 2.3727 | 2.3100–2.4356 | 28/300 |

All 300 paired draws were retained. The paired difference (alternate minus
original) had a median of +0.0283 and a 90% range of +0.0025 to +0.0333.
The bootstrap resamples token-budget settings within each model size and
seeds within 100M-t20. The ranges exclude extrapolation error at 1.15B.

The fitting-method comparison and the test against Team 3's measured loss
remain open.

## Fitting-objective comparison

Reproduce with `python3 team2/rq22_compare_objectives.py`.

For each 50M shape, we fitted each objective on eight completed 50M,
100M, and 200M runs and held out both completed 350M runs. Errors are
predicted minus observed validation loss. We then included the two
350M runs to forecast loss at 1.15B parameters and 23B tokens.

| 50M shape | Objective | 350M-t10 error | 350M-t20 error | Holdout RMSE | Holdout fit hit a bound | 23B forecast |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Original | Log-Huber | -0.0339 | -0.0478 | 0.0415 | Yes | 2.3414 |
| Original | Log-MSE | -0.0355 | -0.0470 | 0.0416 | Yes | 2.3529 |
| Original | Raw-MSE | -0.0344 | -0.0450 | 0.0401 | Yes | 2.3476 |
| 576×9 | Log-Huber | -0.0466 | -0.0525 | 0.0496 | No | 2.3713 |
| 576×9 | Log-MSE | -0.0478 | -0.0598 | 0.0541 | No | 2.3728 |
| 576×9 | Raw-MSE | -0.0478 | -0.0584 | 0.0533 | Yes | 2.3691 |

All six fits underestimated both 350M losses. The lowest holdout RMSE
came from raw-MSE for the original 50M shape and log-Huber for 576×9.
With two holdout runs and a ranking that depends on the shape, we retain
log-Huber for the main forecast and report the other objectives as a
sensitivity check. IsoFLOP and the broader scaling-law method comparison
remain open.

## Exploratory IsoFLOP check

We compared validation loss (`val/loss`) at matched estimated training
compute, using `6 * n_params * tokens_seen`. For each adjacent model pair
and each training horizon, we compared the endpoints and geometric
midpoint of the overlapping compute range. Loss was interpolated linearly
in log compute between logged validation points.

| Model pair | Larger minus smaller loss, t10 | Larger minus smaller loss, t20 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 50M vs 100M | +0.2525 to +0.2934 | +0.1088 to +0.2796 |
| 100M vs 200M | +0.2620 to +0.3445 | +0.1608 to +0.2879 |
| 200M vs 350M | +0.1723 to +0.2305 | +0.1468 to +0.2175 |

The smaller model had lower validation loss in all 18 sampled comparisons.
For duplicate validation records at the same token count, we kept the last
record in the log. The largest duplicate loss spread was 0.001606 for
200M-t10 and 0.001657 for 200M-t20.

At matched compute, checkpoints can be at different stages of their
learning-rate schedules. These comparisons therefore describe the existing
training curves. A dedicated IsoFLOP sweep with schedules set for each
shared compute budget remains open.
